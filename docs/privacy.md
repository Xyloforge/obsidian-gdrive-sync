# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Xyloforge Notes Sync ("the app") is a personal Obsidian plugin that copies notes between the user's Obsidian vault and the user's own Google Drive.

## What the app accesses
- The app requests only the Google Drive scope `drive.file`. This lets it see and change **only the files and folder it created itself**. It cannot see any other file in your Google Drive.
- It reads and writes the notes in the Obsidian vault where it is installed.

## Where your data goes
- Your notes travel directly between your device and Google Drive over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
- There is no server run by the developer. The developer never receives, stores, sees or sells your notes or any other data.
- The app has no analytics, tracking or advertising.
- The Google sign-in token is stored only on your device, inside the vault's plugin settings.

## Google user data
The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the sync feature and is not transferred to anyone.

## Removing access
You can disconnect at any time in the plugin settings, or revoke access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the sync folder in Google Drive removes all copies the app made.
